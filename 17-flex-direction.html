17 - Flex Direction

The flex-direction property controls the direction in which flex items are placed.

1. flex-direction: row

This is the default direction. Items are placed from left to right.

2. flex-direction: row-reverse

Items are placed from right to left.

3. flex-direction: column

Items are placed from top to bottom.
