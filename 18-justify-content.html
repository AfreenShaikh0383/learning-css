18 - Justify Content

The justify-content property controls the alignment and distribution of flex items along the main axis.

1. justify-content: flex-start

Items are placed at the beginning of the main axis.

2. justify-content: flex-end

3. justify-content: center

Items are placed in the center.

1

4. justify-content: space-between

Equal space is placed between the items.

1
2
3

5. justify-content: space-around

Space is placed around each item.

1
2
3

6. justify-content: space-evenly

Equal space is placed between the items and the container edges.
