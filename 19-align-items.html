19 - Align Items

The align-items property controls the alignment of flex items along the cross axis.

1. align-items: stretch

This is the default value. Items stretch to fill the cross axis when possible.

2
3

2. align-items: flex-start

Items are placed at the beginning of the cross axis.

3

3. align-items: flex-end

Items are placed at the end of the cross axis.

1
2
3

4. align-items: center

Items are placed in the center of the cross axis.

1
2
3

5. align-items: baseline

Items are aligned according to their text baseline.
